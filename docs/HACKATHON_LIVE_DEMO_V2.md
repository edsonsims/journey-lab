## ElevenLabs production direction

- **Model:** Eleven v4, `eleven_v4`; requested existing voice: **edson**. Resolve the exact account voice ID at the audio stage. No voice ID or audio generation is claimed in this draft.
- **Delivery:** Edson explaining his own product to a judge. Natural, engaged and confident. Preserve the voice's normal accent. Keep the opening welcoming, the observed problem precise and the result assured.
- **Pace:** roughly 140–150 words per minute while speaking, with room for short breaths and the visible interactions. Aim to finish the closing line around 2:58. Adjust picture holds to the measured take rather than stretching words to arbitrary chapter boundaries.
- **Text:** use only the narration paragraphs, with short sentences and paragraph breaks. Amounts and model version are written as spoken words. Do not send screen directions, timestamps or Markdown to the speech endpoint.
- **Pauses:** natural punctuation; no SSML break tags. Avoid long ellipses, artificial sound effects and theatrical emotional tags. Test pronunciation of Journey Lab, Gather and each Muse name in Edson's voice before the full take.
- **Consistency:** prefer one continuous take for these approximately 2,500 characters, retaining sentence boundaries for later alignment. If a line needs replacement, match voice, settings and surrounding context.
- **Mix:** clear foreground voice, app audio muted. The raw demo has no generated narration. Music, if added in the final edit, must remain secondary to spoken evidence.
- **Timing:** the 180-second schedule is not an audio-duration guarantee. Measure the real ElevenLabs take and generate captions from the final audio timing.

The model ID and speech guidance were checked against [ElevenLabs models](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/models) and [Eleven v4 prompting guidance](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/text-to-speech/best-practices).
