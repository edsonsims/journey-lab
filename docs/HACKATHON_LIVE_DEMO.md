## ElevenLabs production direction

- **Model:** Eleven v4, `eleven_v4`; existing account voice: **Edson**. Both were selected and verified in the signed-in ElevenLabs web app. The production take is retained as `outputs/hackathon-live-demo/audio/edson-paced-take-01.mp3`.
- **Delivery:** Edson showing another developer what he built. Relaxed, conversational and matter-of-fact. Preserve his normal voice and accent, use contractions, and let emphasis follow what he is pointing at. Avoid an announcer or sales-pitch cadence.
- **Pace:** relaxed speech with short breaths and time to read the visible interactions. The generated take measured 122.906 seconds. Sentence groups were aligned to the clicks using pitch- and formant-preserving tempo adjustment; the closing line ends around 2:58.2. The final picture edit also adjusts the finding-detail, patch and booking-confirmation cuts to match the narration.
- **Text:** use only the narration paragraphs, with short sentences and paragraph breaks. Amounts and model version are written as spoken words. Do not send screen directions, timestamps or Markdown to the speech endpoint.
- **Pauses:** natural punctuation; no SSML break tags. Avoid long ellipses, artificial sound effects and theatrical emotional tags. Test pronunciation of Journey Lab, Gather and each Muse name in Edson's voice before the full take.
- **Consistency:** prefer one continuous take for these approximately 2,500 characters, retaining sentence boundaries for later alignment. If a line needs replacement, match voice, settings and surrounding context.
- **Mix:** clear foreground voice, app audio muted, no music. The final audio is mastered for consistent speech loudness; the silent raw demo remains available.
- **Timing:** the exported video is exactly 180 seconds. Captions use a blind local transcription's word timings and the revised script's spelling. The final Casey comparison holds a genuine recorded confirmation frame for 5.9 seconds while Edson describes the paired emotional arcs.

The model ID and speech guidance were checked against [ElevenLabs models](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/models) and [Eleven v4 prompting guidance](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/text-to-speech/best-practices).
